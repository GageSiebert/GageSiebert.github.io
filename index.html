I'm a PhD candidate in astrophysics at Arizona State University, working with Sara Walker on applied algorithmic complexity. My thesis is about the languages we use to describe computation: formalizing interpretable, substrate-specific languages of computation, building software that makes complexity-theoretic analyses practical, and using these tools to find hierarchical structure in biological systems.

After my PhD I want to work on AI safety, which I think is among the most important things one can work on right now.

Before ASU, I studied physics and math at UW–Madison, where I worked on radio cosmology, millisecond pulsars, the optics of a NASA balloon microwave telescope, and the origin of life.
